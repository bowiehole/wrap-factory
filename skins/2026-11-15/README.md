# 2026-11-15 — Heat Map

**heat-map** — Heat Map

**Brief:** Heat Map for the whole car — the car as a thermal camera sees it, in a bold ironbow false-color gradient: a cool deep-indigo roof warms through violet, magenta and red-orange into glowing yellow and white-hot at the rockers, wheel arches and nose, with soft drifting heat blooms across every panel. One continuous color field laid out in world meters, so the glow flows unbroken over the hood, nose, roof and tail. Original design; no logos or text.

# flame-stitch — 2026-11-14

**Title:** Flame Stitch

**Brief:** Flame Stitch for the whole car — a 1970s bargello needlepoint blown up to body size: wavy ribbons of emerald, violet, saffron, hot pink and cream stitched into tight zigzag peaks, split by fine black lines and rolling in big swells from nose to tail. Laid out in surface meters, so the stitch rows run unbroken over the hood, nose, roof and tail.

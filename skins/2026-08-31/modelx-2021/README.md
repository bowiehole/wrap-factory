## Tesla upload steps

- Mobile app (v4.59.0+): Creations → Wrap → Upload
- USB: format exFAT / FAT32 / MS-DOS FAT / ext3 / ext4 (not NTFS). Root folder named Wraps. PNG inside. No map or firmware files on that drive.
- In the car: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps tab
- Limits: official template size, PNG, max 1 MB, up to 10 wraps from the app and 10 from USB

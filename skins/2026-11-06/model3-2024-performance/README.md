# stained-glass — 2026-11-06

**Title:** Stained Glass

**Vehicle:** Model 3 2024 Performance

**Brief:** Stained Glass for the whole car — a cathedral-window tessellation of big irregular glass cells in flat cobalt, ruby, amber, emerald and violet, separated by thick near-black lead lines and merging into large plain black lead areas for breathing room. Cells keep their real size in surface meters around the doors, corners, hood and deck, and the lower body stays plain lead black. Flat colors, no shading. Original design; no logos.

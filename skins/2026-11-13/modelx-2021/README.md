# op-swell — 2026-11-13

**Title:** Op Swell

**Vehicle:** Model X 2021

**Brief:** Op Swell for the whole car — a crisp 1960s op-art checkerboard of ink-black and bone-white squares laid out in surface meters, swelling into two glowing spheres along each flank where the grid balloons outward in cobalt and lemon-to-tangerine and squeezes tight at each rim. The grid runs unbroken over the hood, nose, roof and tail. Original design; no logos or text.

# acid-drip — 2026-11-23

**Title:** Acid Drip

**Vehicle:** Model Y 2025 Premium

**Brief:** Acid Drip for the whole car — acid-lime paint poured over the roof, hood and deck runs down a deep grape body in drips of uneven length, each ending in a round glossy bead, with flung droplets across both colors. Laid out in surface meters so the drips hang straight down every door, corner and bumper and the pour edge stays continuous over the hood, nose and tail. Original design; no logos or text.

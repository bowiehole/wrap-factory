# riso-overprint — 2026-11-28

**Title:** Riso Overprint

**Brief:** Riso Overprint for the whole car — a two-color risograph poster blown up to body size: huge flat discs, rings, fat bars and triangles printed in fluorescent pink and riso blue on a sunflower-yellow sheet, turning deep violet wherever the two inks overprint, laid out in surface meters so every shape keeps one size across the doors, corners, hood, nose and tail. Original design; no logos or text.

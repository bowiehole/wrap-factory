# miami-deco — 2026-11-05

**Title:** Miami Deco

**Vehicle:** Model S 2021

**Brief:** Miami Deco for the whole car — large pastel Art Deco sunburst fans and stepped ziggurat pediments scattered with generous navy space between them over a deep night-navy body. Fans use flat flamingo, aqua, coral and cream wedge rays; ziggurats step those same pastels with thin gold only on the tread edges. Motifs sit in real surface meters so they keep size around the doors, corners, hood and deck, and the lower body stays plain navy. Flat colors, no shading. Original design; no logos.

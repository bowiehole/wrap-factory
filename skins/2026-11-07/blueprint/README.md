# blueprint — 2026-11-07

**Title:** Blueprint

**Brief:** Blueprint for the whole car — a cyanotype construction drawing of sparse white waterlines, dimension ticks and large circle callouts with crosshairs on deep Prussian blue. Marks keep real size in surface meters around the doors, corners, hood and deck, with big plain-blue breathing room. Flat colors, no shading. Original design; no logos.

# moire-ripple — 2026-10-29

**Title:** Moiré Ripple

**Vehicle:** Model Y 2025 Base

**Brief:** Op-art interference for the whole car: two sets of invisible ripples spread out from points ahead of and behind the car, and wherever their rings cross the color flips between deep cobalt and lemon yellow. The result is a bold moiré of checkered arcs that swell, bend and shrink as they travel down the sides and over the hood, nose and tailgate without a break.

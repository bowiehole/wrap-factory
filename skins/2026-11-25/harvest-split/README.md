# harvest-split — 2026-11-25

**Title:** Harvest Split

**Brief:** Harvest Split for the whole car — a retro harvest-poster two-tone: warm cream upper body over deep cranberry lower body, split by one continuous band of pressed autumn leaves (maple, oak and elm in rust, ochre and forest green on chocolate) edged with gold keylines and pinstripes that rings the car at one height across the doors, corners, nose and tail. A few falling leaves drift across the hood and roof.

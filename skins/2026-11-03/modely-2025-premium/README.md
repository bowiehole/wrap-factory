# de-stijl — 2026-11-03

**Title:** De Stijl

**Vehicle:** Model Y 2025 Premium

**Brief:** A De Stijl composition for the whole car — thick black lines meet at right angles on a warm white body, with a few big flat blocks of primary red, yellow and blue set far apart. Lines run level along the doors, around the corners and up over the hood and deck; some stop short at a crossing line, the way the old neoplastic paintings do. Few big clean shapes, no shading. Original design; no logos or reproductions of any specific painting.

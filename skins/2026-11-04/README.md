# 2026-11-04 — Citrus Slice

**citrus-slice** — Citrus Slice

**Brief:** Citrus slices for the whole car — big, flat cross-cut wheels of orange, lemon, lime and pink grapefruit, scattered with plenty of room between them over a deep teal body. Each slice has a bold rind, a thin cream pith ring and juicy wedge segments split by clean cream spokes. Slices are laid out in real surface meters, so they stay round and keep their size along the doors, around the corners and over the hood and deck, and the lower body stays plain teal. Flat colors, no shading. Original design; no logos.

# squiggle-pop — 2026-11-16

**Title:** Squiggle Pop

**Vehicle:** Model Y

**Brief:** Squiggle Pop for the whole car — a loud 1980s Memphis-design print: a bright warm-white body packed with bold black squiggles, cobalt and hot-pink zigzags, pink, yellow and teal triangles with black drop shadows, dotted circles, rings, striped patches and fine black confetti dashes. Every shape is laid out at real size in surface meters, so the pattern runs unbroken over the hood, nose, roof and tail. Original design; no logos or text.

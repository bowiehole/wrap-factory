# rally-livery — 2026-11-09

**Title:** Rally Livery

**Brief:** Rally Livery for the whole car — a bright chalk-white body over a lagoon-teal lower half that sweeps up from a low nose into a tall rear haunch, traced by a hot-coral and graphite double pinstripe, with twin coral center stripes edged in graphite running nose to tail over the hood, roof and deck. Laid out in world meters so the split and stripes stay continuous around the corners and across the hood-to-nose crease. Flat colors. Original design; no logos, numbers or sponsor marks.

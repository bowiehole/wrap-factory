# 2026-11-10 — Poppy Field

**poppy-field** — Poppy Field

**Brief:** Poppy Field for the whole car — a remembrance field of bold red four-petal poppies with black centers rising from a deep olive meadow of grass blades along the rocker, dense and large low on the doors and thinning upward into a pale dawn sky that warms from soft blue-gray to cream at the roofline, with a few blooms drifting onto the hood, roof and deck. Blooms are placed in world meters so they stay round on the hood and wrap continuously around the nose, tail and corners. Original design; no flags, insignia, logos or text.

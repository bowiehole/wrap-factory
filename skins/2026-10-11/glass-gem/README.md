# glass-gem — 2026-10-11

**Title:** Glass Gem

**Brief:** Indigenous Peoples' Day eve — an impression of jewel-toned glass-gem corn, honoring the Indigenous farmers who first bred maize: the whole car packed edge to edge with rounded translucent kernels in indigo, turquoise, ruby, amber, jade, rose and pearl, each with a soft inner glow and a small glint, set in thin near-black seams; world-meter 3D mosaic so kernels stay round on the hood and run unbroken over the hood-to-fascia crease, door seams, roof and tail; dense coverage, regenerated impression, no logos or tribal symbols.
